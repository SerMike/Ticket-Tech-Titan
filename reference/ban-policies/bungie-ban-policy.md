# Bungie Game Account Restrictions and Banning Policy

**Sources:**
- https://safety.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/42308518654100-Bungie-Game-Account-Restrictions-and-Banning-Policies
- https://help.bungie.net/hc/en-us/articles/360049517431-Destiny-Account-Restrictions-and-Banning-Policies

## Account Bans vs. Restrictions

**Ban:** A player has permanently lost account access to the game.

**Restriction:** A player has temporarily lost access to specific activities. The restriction message indicates which activities are impacted and the duration. Players may resume participation once the restriction expires.

**Device Ban:** No account will be able to access Bungie games on that banned device, regardless of the individual account ban status. Players who purchase a used console or PC that has previously been device-banned will not be able to connect to Bungie games using that device.

> **Important:** Any ban or restriction for a player determined to be cheating or violating the Code of Conduct may also be applied to a team if the team benefited from the cheating player. Players should exercise caution when joining teams with unknown players and should leave the activity if they suspect a player on their team is cheating.

## Reasons for a Ban

### Frequent Quitting or Removal
Players who frequently quit or are removed from matches may receive a restriction or ban.

### Poor Connection Quality
Bungie will issue restrictions to accounts that have consistently unstable connections when playing, as this negatively impacts other players.

### Third-Party PC Applications
Bungie resists attempts by third-party applications to insert code into game clients. Running incompatible applications may result in bans. Common apps like Discord, XSplit, OBS, and RTSS are permitted. Third-party applications used to violate the Code of Conduct or license agreement are not.

### Abuse of External Accessibility Tools
Abusing external accessibility tools or aids specifically to gain an advantage over other players will result in escalating actions, including bans.

- "External accessibility aides" = any device or input that augments the player's ability to control the game beyond what it normally provides (programmable controllers, keyboard/mouse adapters, advanced macros, AI automation). Does not include features Bungie provides.
- Violation = using these tools to mitigate challenges all players face (e.g., reducing recoil, increasing aim assist) or to automate difficult/prestigious content.
- Not a violation = using an accessibility aide to play the game where a player could not play otherwise.

Players detected using automation to circumvent idle detection or perform automated activities to earn loot (XP, credits, materials, equipment, drops) may be restricted or banned.

### Win Trading
Intentionally granting other players wins in PvP activities. Includes manipulating matchmaking by any means to force-match with specific opponents or coordinating with opponents to provide uncontested victories. Bungie issues bans to players actively engaging in win trading.

### Account Recoveries
Account recoveries = giving someone else access to your platform account and control of your characters. Participating as a buyer or seller of account recovery services could result in a ban.

**Will NOT get your account banned:**
- Letting a family member or friend play casually on your account
- Sharing an account on a common console
- Letting a friend try out a weapon roll

**Could get your account banned or restricted:**
- Charging others to recover their account for boosting, earning items, completing content, etc.
- Recovering accounts in exchange for anything of value (Patreon, subscriptions, gift cards, etc.)
- Paying someone to recover your account to be boosted or have items earned
- Boosting recoveries done for free
- Letting a friend recover your account who then boosts or cheats on it without your knowledge or consent

### Code of Conduct or License Agreement Violation
Prohibited behaviors include, but are not limited to:

**Modified Software/Hardware:**
- Connecting with modified game software, game assets, hardware, console firmware, or operating system files (including emulators and virtual machines)

**External Programs:**
- Using an external program to modify or analyze game software operation: trainers, mods, cheats, aimbots, autofire, user mode or kernel debuggers, graphics hacks
- Sending malicious network traffic to Bungie servers or other players
- Using an external program or device to automate gameplay or circumvent idle detection
- Modifying or interrupting game network traffic to disrupt other players or gain an advantage

**Other Violations:**
- Intentionally deriving gameplay benefit from another user's prohibited behavior
- Unauthorized access to game software or downloadable content (piracy)
- Impersonating a Bungie, Sony, Microsoft, Valve, or Epic Games employee or Bungie partner
- Hate speech, griefing, or message spamming
- Habitual unsportsmanlike conduct
- Habitual quitting or idle behavior in matchmade activities
- Habitually quitting from competitive game modes
- Intentionally deleting another player's items or characters by logging in with their account credentials

## Enforcement Tiers

- **Restrictions** — Temporary; may not be overturned or undone before they expire.
- **Bans** — Permanent account bans are the result of escalating restrictions for the same behavior or for extreme violations.
- **Device Bans** — Activity bans or restrictions may be escalated to device bans after multiple confirmed offenses.
- **First-Time Egregious Violations** — Bungie reserves the right to permanently ban accounts or player hardware for first-time offenses deemed to be egregious violations of the Code of Conduct or Terms of Use.
- **Cross-Game Risk** — Players who have ever been banned or restricted in any Bungie game are considered high-risk and are eligible for bans and restrictions at lower thresholds than players who have never been banned.

> **Important:** Bans and restrictions are not applied automatically. Bungie reviews all possible violations before applying a ban or restriction to an account or device.

---

## Restricted Access to Gear, Items, and Content

When losing access to specific activities, players may also lose access to gear and items associated with that activity, including Destiny/Destiny 2 and Marathon purchases, weapons, armor, items, quests, or game modes.

> Players may lose access to all gear associated with an activity that they have been banned or restricted from, even if that gear was received outside the activity.

---

## Appeal Process

Players who have received a ban or restriction may contact Bungie using the Ban/Restriction Appeal Contact Form.

- Permanent account bans and temporary restrictions are only implemented with rigorous checking by trained Bungie employees.
- Restrictions are temporary and may not be overturned or undone before they expire.
- Bungie takes actions with evidence that a reasonable person could conclude that cheating was knowingly taking place.
- Players are responsible for their online account, gaming hardware, and any actions taken in their games.

---

## Legitimate Behaviors (Will NOT Result in a Ban)

- Playing the game normally in any way the player finds fun
- Using static analysis to reverse engineer data formats for the purpose of viewing game assets (note: runtime analysis is NOT permitted)
- Publishing or sharing information about Bungie games via non-commercial means
- Accessing the publicly-facing Bungie.net and Companion web APIs in a manner that does not create unreasonable load
- Discovering a game exploit and reporting it (players will not be punished for finding exploits)

---

## Additional Notes

- **Steam Deck / Steam Play Proton:** Destiny 2 and Marathon are not supported on SteamOS/Proton. Attempting to bypass this incompatibility will result in an account ban.
- **Error Code Weasel:** Received when a ban or restriction is applied. Note that most instances of this error are unrelated to bans.
- **Cross-Title Restrictions:** A restriction or ban in one Bungie game does not automatically carry over to other Bungie games, unless a separate ban is applied for that game.
