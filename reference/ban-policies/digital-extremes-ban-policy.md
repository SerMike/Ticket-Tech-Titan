# Digital Extremes (Warframe) Ban & Account Restriction Policy

**Sources:**
- https://support.warframe.com/hc/en-us/articles/44241767300749-Digital-Extremes-Code-of-Conduct-Guidelines
- https://www.warframe.com/en/terms

---

## Code of Conduct & Community Standards

The guidelines are designed to make Warframe communities an entertaining, interesting, and safe place. They work in tandem with the End User License Agreement (EULA) and Terms of Service (TOS).

When interacting in a Digital Extremes community, conversations should be:
- Thoughtful and respectful
- Stay on-topic
- Constructive and descriptive
- Avoid rage, rants, name-and-shame, spam, advertisements, and inappropriate content

Respecting others means not insulting, bullying, or harassing anyone. This applies to in-game activity (chat, companion and weapon names, user-generated content) and official Digital Extremes platforms (forums, Discord, streaming platforms, social media).

---

## Zero-Tolerance Policy

If found posting slurs, hate-speech, or any form of hate speech (including self-censored, masked, or rearranged versions), accounts will be banned immediately.

---

## Enforcement Tiers (Warning Point System)

| Warning Points | Penalty |
|---|---|
| 3 points | 3-day suspension + 30-day moderation queue |
| 4 points | 7-day suspension + 1-year moderation queue |
| 5 points | Permanent suspension |

Warning points are automatically removed after one year from when they are first issued.

---

## Broad Discretionary Authority (from Terms of Use)

> "Digital Extremes may at its option issue warnings and temporary suspensions and permanent terminations of accounts for user violations. Digital Extremes retains the sole discretion as to when and how to impose warnings, penalties and/or disciplinary actions. DE considers the severity of the violation and the number of infractions in making the determination; however, any determination shall be under the absolute discretion of DE."

> "Digital Extremes always retains the right to terminate or suspend a user's account at any time for any reason, or for no reason, with or without any warnings and with or without notice."

---

## Appeal Process

Players who believe they have been wrongfully banned can submit a support ticket at:
https://support.warframe.com/hc/en-us/sections/200020700-General-Account-Support

A member of the Digital Extremes team will review the reason for the penalty with them.
