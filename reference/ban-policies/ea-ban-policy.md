## Types of Account Actions

### Account Locks
Temporary security measure when EA detects suspicious activity like multiple failed login attempts. This is a protective measure, not a violation penalty.

### Suspensions
Temporarily remove access to a game, part of a game, or the entire EA Account when activity violates the Rules of Conduct. Suspensions are timed and expire after a set period (minimum 7 days, maximum 365 days). Typically issued as warnings for first/minor violations.

### Bans
Remove access to a game, part of a game, or EA Account for an extended period or permanently. Usually issued for repeated violations or extreme/severe violations. Account bans are typically permanent.

## Prohibited Behaviors

### Cheating
- Using third-party software such as aimbots, wallhacks, and other similar cheats to gain unfair advantage
- Performing in-game exploits or abusing glitches/bugs
- Stat padding (artificially stacking scores)
- Win-trading or manipulating in-game ranking systems with others
- Using software or cheat tools that disconnect other players from EA servers
- Using unauthorized hardware, accessories, or modifications

### Harassment and Disruptive Behavior
- Posting vulgar, threatening, harassing, abusive, hateful, defamatory, offensive, or obscene content
- Bullying, sexual harassment, trolling, stalking, insults, or slurs
- Sharing non-consensual intimate images
- Impersonating another person, group, or EA employee
- Disrupting normal gameplay through excessive shouting (all caps), spamming, or flooding chat

### Illegal/Harmful Content
- Violent threats or threats to physically harm others
- Promoting or inciting violence
- Sharing another person's personally identifiable information (PII)
- Sexual content, pornography, nudity, or graphic sexual acts

### Technical Violations
- Hacking, cracking, or using software that damages or interferes with EA Services
- Denial of service attacks
- Uploading computer viruses, worms, Trojan horses, or spyware
- Interfering with servers or networks

### Account/Business Violations
- Selling EA accounts or entitlements without permission
- Abusing refund systems
- Violating local laws or regulations

## Enforcement Tiers

### Minor Violations
- 7-day to 30-day temporary suspension
- First offense typically results in a warning/temporary ban to allow players to change behavior

### Repeated/Severe Violations
- Extended temporary suspensions (up to 365 days)
- Permanent account ban
- If a player receives a temporary ban and continues the same behavior, subsequent bans may be permanent

### Severe or Illegal Violations (No Warning)
- Permanent ban issued immediately without prior warning
- Examples include: hacking/cracking, selling accounts, extreme harassment, threatening illegal activities, high-risk or illegal content
- These violations do not expire and cannot be appealed for lesser penalties

### Multiple Accounts
- If a player has multiple EA Accounts, depending on violation type or misuse, EA may terminate all accounts and related entitlements

## Appeal Process

1. Use the **Penalty History tool** in EA Account settings
2. Select the violation to appeal
3. Submit appeal through the form

EA will send an email explaining their decision. Response time is typically 10–30 days, up to 5–6 weeks during high-volume periods.
